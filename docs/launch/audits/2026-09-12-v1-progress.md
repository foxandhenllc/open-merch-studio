# V1 implementation checkpoint — September 12, 2026

Open Merch Studio remains unreleased. These are committed source improvements and local rehearsals;
no production deployment, live payment, provider order, image generation, or live-storage deletion
was performed for this checkpoint. Workspace package versions remain 0.1.0.

## What the owner can now do

| Outcome | Source checkpoint | Why it matters to a creator or community brand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enable an approved collection and take it through a complete fixture purchase | `a7265e0` | Upload original artwork, approve prices and print layouts, and offer a focused collection. Orders retain the exact prepared prints independently of later collection edits. |
| Choose Flare or Sunburst with migrated settings and reconciled usage estimates | `9b2e90e` | Model changes remain an owner control. Legacy Image 2/remove.bg behavior is retired, imported artwork is retained, and uncertain provider usage does not silently restore the allowance. |
| Review orders and inspect their saved prints in admin | `3bbd652` | The owner can acknowledge an issue, record its resolution, and explicitly retry eligible draft preparation without editing code. Manual production review remains mandatory. |
| Review and clear abandoned print preparations | `1db3210` | A seven-day post-expiry grace period, protected order references, and retryable deletion records make storage maintenance part of the owner workflow. |

The final review fix at `9e046dc` keeps refunded orders with unresolved fulfillment issues in
**Needs attention**. Its desktop/phone regression check distinguishes unresolved exceptions from
resolved refunds and ordinary delivered orders. The branded-install receipts above apply to
`1db3210`; this subsequent UI-only fix has separate lint/type/unit/build and browser evidence.

The resulting demonstration follows one understandable story: an owner publishes their artwork,
a customer buys the approved product, and the owner reviews the exact files for that order.
The artist, creator, and local-business examples can reuse this foundation after the V1 gates pass.
This checkpoint does not add Twitch ingestion, community submissions/voting, membership billing,
or new persona repositories.

## Work still required before V1

The [release contract](../v1-release-contract.md) remains authoritative. The remaining implementation
work is guided/resumable first installation and recovery, logo/share-image and domain handling,
server-enforced collection personalization, older-order navigation beyond the latest 100 orders,
and closing the supported template/setup details.
Those are engineering tasks, not evidence that an owner must intervene in every development step.

Separately, release evidence still needs a real physical sample and authorized payment/fulfillment
lifecycle, account-specific AI quality/cost calibration, a complete database-plus-artwork restore
rehearsal, and an observed nontechnical owner setup/publication/recovery attempt. Record actual
assistance and elapsed time before advertising quick self-service or a practically automatic store.

Run the final acceptance checks against the eventual release candidate, complete release review,
and only then change versions/tag/publish V1. Build client-style persona forks from that exact tag.
